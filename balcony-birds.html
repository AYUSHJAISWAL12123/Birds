Water and cover

  • A shallow dish, 3 to 4 cm deep, with a flat stone in the middle so small birds can stand. Refill daily and scrub it weekly so it does not breed mosquitoes.
  • Keep the dish about 1 to 2 metres from your densest plant so birds have cover to dash into.
  • Skip pesticides on this balcony. Parents feed chicks on insects, and sprays remove them.
